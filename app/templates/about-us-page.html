MADE BY CARLS, FOR CARLS

A campus worth keeping close.

Carls Chat started as a project in Carleton’s Advanced Software Design class. We wanted to capture the little things that make campus feel like home: a conversation on the Bald Spot, a coffee at Sayles, a favorite floor in Gould. So we drew our own little Carleton and made a place to meet.
